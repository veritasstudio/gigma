Built for making music together

Gigma sits on a channel in your DAW. Your mix keeps passing through untouched while a copy streams out to the room.

Lives in your DAW

Load it as AU or VST3 on any channel. It passes your audio straight through and streams a copy to your guests.

Guests just open a link

They type a name in a phone or desktop browser and tap Listen. No install, no account, no password.

Talk back

Guests unmute their browser mic and you hear them in your DAW. Browser guests hear each other too.

Plugin to plugin, anywhere

Producers with the plugin join each other over the internet through the Gigma relay. No port forwarding, no VPN.

Tune it to your network

Pick Low latency, Balanced or Stable in the plugin. More buffer means fewer dropouts on a weak connection.

See who is in the room

The plugin shows who is connected, and the room page shows who is listening and who is talking.

How it works

Three steps from a blank session to everyone listening.

  1. 1

    Install the plugin

    Download it below and add Gigma to the channel you want to share, usually the master or a bus.

  2. 2

    Start a meeting, share the link

    In the plugin, choose New meeting (host) and copy the link. Send it on WhatsApp, Messages or anywhere.

  3. 3

    Guests join and listen

    Guests open the link, type a name and tap Listen. They can unmute to talk. Plugin users choose Join as plugin instead.

Questions

Do guests need an account or an app?

No. Guests open the link in a phone or desktop browser, type a name and tap Listen. There is no login and no install.

Should everyone wear headphones?

Yes, please. If a guest unmutes without headphones, their mic picks up the music from their speakers and you hear your own track coming back.

How much delay is there?

There is always some. Browser guests hear you about a quarter of a second behind your DAW. Plugin to plugin goes through our relay, so it depends on both connections and is usually a hundred milliseconds or more. That works well for sharing ideas, reviewing a mix and loose jamming. It is not meant for playing tightly in time together.

My connection is weak. What can I do?

Open the plugin's quality setting and switch from Balanced to Stable. It adds buffer so you get fewer crackles and dropouts, at the cost of a little more delay. Wired internet or a strong Wi-Fi signal helps most.

Which DAWs does it work with?

The Mac build includes AU and VST3. The Windows build is VST3, for hosts like FL Studio, Ableton Live, Reaper and Cubase. Any DAW that loads these formats should be able to use it.

Can two producers connect plugin to plugin?

Yes. The second producer pastes your meeting link into the plugin and chooses Join as plugin. Audio goes through the Gigma relay, so nobody needs to open a port or set up a VPN.
